2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit this website, place an order, subscribe to our emails, or contact us — and what we do with it. It applies to this website and to our customer care channels.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

We share data with service providers who process it on our instructions and only for the purposes above:

  • Payment processing
  • Order fulfilment, warehousing, and delivery carriers
  • Email and customer support platforms
  • Website hosting and content delivery
  • Website analytics

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to, or to establish or defend legal claims.

6. International transfers

We are based in Singapore, and some of our service providers are located outside your country. Where we transfer personal data across borders, we take steps required by the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) — and, where the GDPR applies, we rely on an adequacy decision or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses — so that your data receives a comparable standard of protection.

7. How long we keep it

  • Order and transaction records: retained for the period required by Singapore tax and accounting law (currently 5 years from the end of the relevant financial year).
  • Account and subscription data: for as long as your account is active, then archived.
  • Marketing contacts: until you unsubscribe, and then a minimal suppression record so we do not email you again.
  • Customer service messages: up to 24 months after your enquiry is closed.

8. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep your cart and session working, to remember your preferences, and — with your consent where required — to measure how the site is used. Strictly necessary cookies cannot be switched off without breaking checkout. You can block or delete other cookies through your browser settings.

9. Marketing

We only send marketing emails if you have asked us to. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also email care@doctorshabit.com at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect transactional emails about an order you have placed.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • ask us to delete data we no longer need;
  • withdraw consent, including for marketing;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • ask us to transfer your data to another provider.

To exercise any of these, email care@doctorshabit.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first, and some requests are limited where we must retain records by law.

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please contact us first so we can put it right. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission of Singapore at pdpc.gov.sg, or, if you are in the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.

12. Security

This website is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection and payments are handled by PCI-DSS compliant providers. We restrict access to personal data to staff and providers who need it. No system is completely secure, but if a data breach is likely to result in significant harm to you, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

13. Children

Our products and this website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “last updated” date at the top shows when. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will tell you by email or a notice on the website before it takes effect.
