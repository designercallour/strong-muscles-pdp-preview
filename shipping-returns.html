1. Where we ship

We dispatch all orders from Singapore. Delivery is available to the destinations listed at checkout. If your country does not appear at checkout, we are not currently able to ship to you.

You are the importer of record for your order. Any customs duties, import taxes, or handling fees charged by your country are your responsibility and are not included in the price shown at checkout.

2. Dispatch and delivery times

Orders placed before 2:00pm Singapore time (GMT+8) on a business day are normally dispatched the same day. Orders placed after that, or on a weekend or Singapore public holiday, are dispatched the next business day.

Destination Estimated delivery Cost
Singapore 1–3 business days after dispatch Free
Rest of world 7–14 business days after dispatch Calculated at checkout

Delivery estimates are estimates, not guarantees. Once a parcel is handed to the carrier, transit time is outside our control. Customs inspection, severe weather, and carrier backlogs can extend delivery beyond the ranges above.

3. Tracking

You will receive a dispatch confirmation email with a tracking number once your order leaves our warehouse. If tracking has not updated for seven consecutive days, contact care@doctorshabit.com and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

4. Lost, delayed, or damaged parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged, photograph the outer packaging and the contents before discarding anything and email us within 7 days of delivery. If your parcel is confirmed lost by the carrier, we will replace it at no cost to you or refund it in full, at your choice.

If a parcel is returned to us because the delivery address was incomplete or incorrect, or because delivery was refused or went unclaimed, we will refund the price of the goods less the original outbound shipping cost.

5. Returns

5.1 Change of mind — unopened goods

You may return unopened, unused products in their original sealed packaging within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the purchase price. Return postage for change-of-mind returns is paid by you.

5.2 Faulty, damaged, or incorrect goods

If a product arrives faulty, damaged, past its expiry date, or is not what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery. We will arrange a replacement or a full refund including all shipping costs, and we will pay for return postage. This does not affect your statutory rights under Singapore’s Consumer Protection (Fair Trading) Act or any equivalent consumer law that applies where you live.

5.3 Goods we cannot accept back

For health and safety reasons we cannot accept returns of products where the outer seal or an individual sachet has been opened, unless the product is faulty or was sent in error. This is a food supplement and we cannot resell or safely re-stock opened goods.

6. How to start a return

  1. Email care@doctorshabit.com with your order number and the reason for the return.
  2. We will reply with a return authorisation and the correct return address within one business day.
  3. Send the goods back using a tracked service. Keep your proof of postage until the refund lands.

Please do not send returns to our registered office. Parcels sent without a return authorisation may not be traceable to your order and may delay your refund.

7. Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout. We process approved refunds within 5 business days of receiving the returned goods, or of approving a claim where no return is required. Your bank or card issuer may take a further 3–10 business days to show the credit on your statement.

We do not charge restocking fees. Where a return is due to our error, you will never be out of pocket for postage.

8. Subscriptions

Subscription plans renew automatically at the interval shown on the product page and at checkout, at the price confirmed in your order confirmation email, until you cancel.

  • You can pause, reschedule, change, or cancel a subscription at any time, with no cancellation fee and no minimum number of deliveries.
  • Changes must be made at least 2 business days before your next scheduled dispatch. After that point the order has entered fulfilment and cannot be changed.
  • We email you before each renewal so a delivery never arrives unannounced.
  • To cancel, email care@doctorshabit.com or use the link in any subscription email.

If a subscription renewal is dispatched after you asked us to cancel, we will refund it in full and you do not need to return the goods.
